Features

Built for Windows and Android.

One direct remote-control app across your PC and phone.

PC to PC

Sit at one PC. Work on another.

Picture, sound, keyboard and mouse, quick enough to play on. The sign-in screen too, if you want it.

  • A screen that matches the one in front of you
  • The other monitor stays dark while you work
  • Copy, paste and files, in both directions

From a Windows PC today. The Android version is still being tested.

Phone

Your phone, on your desk.

Android apps and games in a window, with your keys and mouse standing in for your thumbs.

  • A mapping editor made for games
  • The phone's own screen can stay dark
  • Over your own Wi-Fi or hotspot
Anywhere

Leave home. Stay one click away.

Turn it on once. After that your devices find your PC and connect to it directly.

  • Ten digits pair a device that is far away
  • Off until you turn it on
  • If it cannot connect, it tells you why
Private

Straight from one device to the other. Nothing in between.

No account, and no Mizar server. What you see and do stays between your two devices.

  • Nothing connects until you allow it
  • No analytics, no ads
  • Uninstalls without a trace
