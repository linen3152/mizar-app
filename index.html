Signing in with Google

Optional, and not released yet. Sign in, and each of your devices can see the others.

Mizar asks Google for your identity, your e-mail address, and a private folder of its own in your Google Drive, where it keeps one thing: an encrypted list of your devices.

It cannot see your other files. Nothing is sent to us, or to anyone else.

Mizar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
