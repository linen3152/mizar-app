Reach a PC from anywhere

One switch, and what to try when something is in the way.

Turn it on

  1. On the PC, open This PC.
  2. Turn on Reach this PC from anywhere and confirm.

It is off until you do this. Only PCs can be reached this way; a phone accepts connections from its own Wi-Fi or hotspot only.

What the status means

StatusMeaning
ReachableYour devices can connect from most networks
LimitedIt works from some networks. The card says what to change
Not reachableThe PC cannot be found from outside. The card says why
Waiting for a deviceNothing is paired yet

Connection check, under the card, shows what is behind the status.

If it cannot connect

Mizar connects your devices directly, and a few networks do not allow that. The message says where it stopped. Things that usually help:

  • Switch the device you hold to another network, for example mobile data.
  • Turn on IPv6 on your home router, if your provider offers it.
  • Let your home router open ports automatically (often called UPnP).
  • If your devices already share a VPN, connect over it.
  • Set the clock to automatic on both devices.

What the outside world can see while this is on is in the privacy policy.
