Clipboard and files

Copy here, paste there. Move files either way.

Clipboard

Copy on one device, paste on the other. It is on by default when you connect to a PC, and you can switch it off in the quick menu.

  • It works while the session window is the one you are using, and pauses in game mode.
  • Passwords copied from a password manager are left alone.

Send files

  • From Windows: drop them on the session window.
  • From Android: use Send files, or share to Mizar from another app.

They arrive in Downloads\Mizar on the PC.

Get files

Open File transfer in the quick menu, browse the PC's Desktop, Downloads or Documents, and pick a file or a folder. It lands in your own Downloads\Mizar.

Good to know

  • Nothing is overwritten, and nothing received is opened for you.
  • A dropped connection picks the transfer up again if it returns within ten minutes.
  • The PC shows the person in front of it that files were fetched.
  • A phone that is being controlled does not offer clipboard or files yet.
