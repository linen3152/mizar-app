What leaves your device

Very little, and never to us.

Stays on your devices

Keys, paired devices, settings and logs. On Windows they live in %ProgramData%\Mizar and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mizar; on Android, in the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded.

Goes to your other device

Your screen, sound, input, clipboard and files, directly and locked, to the device you paired and nowhere else.

Seen by others

Nothing, while remote access is off. With it on, the public services that help your devices find each other see an internet address and nothing of what you do.

Goes to us

Nothing. There is no Mizar server, no account and no analytics.

The complete version is the privacy policy.
