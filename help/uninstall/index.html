Uninstall cleanly

Remove Mizar and everything it installed.

Windows

  1. Open Settings > Apps > Installed apps, find Mizar and choose Uninstall.
  2. Choose keep my data or remove everything.
  3. Approve the Windows prompt.

What goes

ItemRemoved
The program, its shortcut and its entry in Installed appsAlways
The background service, the virtual display driver, the firewall rulesAlways, if Mizar installed them
The port opened on your routerAlways. If the router cannot be reached, it closes by itself within about two hours
Pairings, settings and logsWith "remove everything"

Mizar removes what Mizar installed and touches nothing else. Other Windows accounts on the same PC keep their own Mizar data until they delete it.

To remove just one part, such as the service, use Installed components in the app.

Android

Uninstall the app as usual. Files you received stay in Download/Mizar.

Afterwards

On your other devices, remove this one from Paired devices.
