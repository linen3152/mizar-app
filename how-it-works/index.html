Two devices. One line.

Mizar has no servers of its own.

  • Pair

    Allow a device once, in person or with ten digits.

  • Find

    Public services introduce your devices to each other. That is all they do.

  • Connect

    From there the two talk directly, locked with keys only they hold.

Some networks will not let two devices meet. When that happens, Mizar says so, and says why. The fine detail is in the privacy policy.
